# Research brief template

```text
# <slug> — Research Brief

Written <YYYY-MM-DD>. Pins: <repo short sha the claims were checked against>

## Bottom line
Recommendation (reuse / adapt upstream / built-in / build new), why, and the next step.

## What exists here
Local code, tests and config the change can reuse or must respect. Cite file:line.

## External sources
Only when the change has an external surface (it adds or upgrades a dependency, integrates an external
system, or relies on a version-specific API): official docs (with version) and upstream links.
Omit this section otherwise.

## Options and risks
The alternatives considered, why each lost, and what could go wrong.

## Open questions
Anything that would change the recommendation.
```
